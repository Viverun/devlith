## Artificial Intelligence & Field of Mathematics
*So they've proved the Riemann Hypothesis huh..*

Something seems very off in general about the way Millennium Prize Problems being taken down one by one by these LLMs, Although not very surprising because it was bound to happen one day but that pace is unprecedentedly higher than anyone could've anticipated. Though my experience with LLMs while doing my Computer Under-Grad mathematics have so far been quite "irritating", almost every time I prompt AI to find a solution for a problem, I outrightly reject the answer that it generates because it's simply way too peculiar for my understanding of the subject

But hey this entire **AI saturating Mathematical Problem** benchmark has a deep rooted problem that needs to be addressed with the care it deserves, These AI Labs are knowingly causing the chaos just to make their model seem "**intelligent**" without caring about the level of damage their brute force nature of solution is causing. 
Humans have taken years and if not centuries in building the current knowledge of mathematics which have proportionately resulted in the advancement of Humanity and our understanding of universe itself, we have figured out various problems and their solution which then led to even bigger problems and we continue to hunt solutions for them too.

In other field of study, an incorrect or a wrong solution might've just get ignored or don't get the same level of recognition as their counterpart but it's different in mathematics here
An incorrect proof deserves an equal amount of importance here as the correct one because the human ingenuity brought along with it's reasoning only leads to more progress in this branch and that's what the **romanticism** in mathematics is

But the problem begins when some people decides to act according to their own selfish interest with no passion in this field and only see these problems as a mean of promoting their Public Offering, their approach march to the destruction of hidden mathematics, the human genius element and eventually halt the growth, soon there won't be any problem worth approaching with the same level of enthusiasm as it used to be, nobody knows just how much contribution had been done in the effort to prove RH but now as it's proved, would mathematicians have that same level of competitiveness within them for this problem anymore? I agree that even the fact that RH is true open many doors[*like our understanding of number theory*] now but why would any firm fund mathematicians to work on a problem whose solution already exist? What incentive could that possibly bring around? There is no merit in something that brings no honor and recognition which goes beyond monetary value

The second generalized though seemingly the weaker and uncompleted proof by **Alessandro Masullo** [*human :)*] of Riemann Hypothesis took 12 years but likely insufficient to be even considered as a proof, The statement then followed by Masullo *We are still here. We are very much alive*. I really want to die on that[literally], I am trying my best to be optimistic here but the odds are way against Humans, I am oblivious regarding the way things are moving forward yet critically aware that they are!
