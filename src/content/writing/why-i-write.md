I've started and abandoned more notebooks than I can count. Most of them died because they were *for* something — a course, a project, an interview. This one is different: it's for me.

## Writing is thinking

Most of what I believe I only discover while trying to write it down. A thought that feels complete in my head falls apart the moment I try to put it in a sentence. That's the point.

## What you'll find here

- Notes on what I'm building and learning
- Things I'm feeling and still figuring out
- Mistakes, written down so I make them less often
