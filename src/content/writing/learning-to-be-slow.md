There's a specific feeling I get when I don't understand something. A tightness. An urge to skim, to copy the answer, to move on.

## Sitting with it

Lately I've been trying to stay in that feeling a little longer. To read the same paragraph three times. To draw the diagram by hand.

It's slower. It's also the only way anything has ever actually stuck.
